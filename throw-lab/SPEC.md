# Scope — Throw Lab
Derived from the original conversation and cumulative V11 implementation.
 
## Core experience
A compact monochrome indoor gym devoted to freely throwing physical objects. The ball moves with pointer input, accepts circular spin gestures, and follows a free curved flight after release. There is no creature or aiming-circle target.

## Required content
- Metal bucket, regulation-size basketball hoop/backboard/net, knockable can pyramid.
- Capture shell, four-star orb, bowling ball, basketball, lunar sphere, candy swirl, energy sphere. Each must have appropriate visual form, dimensions, material response, mass, inertia and distinct physical behavior.
- Spin/flick, drag release, timed jump shot with visible shooting movement and adjustable aim, underhand lob, bank shot.
- Meaningful contacts: rim rolling/rattling/rim-outs, board rebounds, bucket lip and interior contacts, net deformation and independent cans.
- Responsive mouse/touch, reliable cancel and reset, stable camera, functioning lighting/shadows, coherent compact gym.
- Preserve cumulative content and controls, no simplified replacement. Integrate into Euclidi Games; use compatible reusable assets/technology where feasible, with provenance and license audit.

## Acceptance
Compare rendered meshes with collider surfaces and scale; test direct swishes, rim-outs, missed shots, bank shots, bucket entries, can tumbles, and each combination of target, ball and technique. Verify left/right curve and release strength, avoid automatic steering, confirm visible light mode changes, and test at portrait and landscape sizes. Calibrate real-world ball properties against documented measurements; label fictional properties estimates. Confirm actual browser playability before describing as verified.
