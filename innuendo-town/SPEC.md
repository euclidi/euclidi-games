# Approved scope
- Retro top-down, free-roaming urban sandbox with eight businesses and sixteen fictional adult residents.
- Opening hours and time progression; personality-driven double entendres, gossip, composure, rapport and reputation.
- Employment, outfits, home ownership and recovery; versioned local persistence.
- Arcade Physics props and delivery tasks; distinct job rules for sorting, timing, pressure, washing and delivery.
- Keyboard, touch and tap navigation.
- Asset-first: authentic reusable sprite artwork, coherent map, native engine, independently test before release.
 
## World autonomy
Resident simulation persists independently of the active scene; needs, travel, work, trade, relationships and generated town news progress without player input. Shops have stock and revenue, and the player may observe resident state. No uncontrolled offline time progression.

## Visual and sound direction
Cohesive native-pixel urban artwork, adult-proportioned directional sprites, complete and correctly facing vehicles, meaningful scene-specific props, subdued shadows and lighting. Readable game-specific interface and mobile controls; locally bundled licensed audio samples and environmental soundscape, with explicit mute. Preserve the simulation and collision map.

## Release gates
Desktop and mobile browser boot; no runtime errors; asset dimensions and frame selection visually reviewed; all eight entrances and job loops; input recovery; save/reload; performance and screenshot review. Publish to Euclidi Games only after these pass.
