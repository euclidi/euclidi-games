# TENPIN — development roadmap

1. Verify WebGL startup and pinned module fetch in desktop and mobile browsers; test straight shots, gutters, pocket strikes, splits, spares, tenth-frame bonus rolls and reset.
2. Tune pin contact hulls, mass distribution, tipping stability, lane friction and hook; compare against observed bowling shots. Improve camera handoff and pinsetter/deadwood sequence.
3. Evaluate CC0 pin/alley asset candidates (OpenGameArt bowling pin and licensed bowling alley sets); acquire and inspect source models before integrating. Preserve retro visual coherence; no generic primitive placeholders in final art. Add verified gameplay cover.
4. Improve audio with licensed recording assets and rolling/impact intensity. Test touch latency, frame time, gamepad mappings and persistence. Ship only after real playtest.

Internal milestones are not approval gates.
