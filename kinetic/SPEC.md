Owner approval: 2026-11-27. Preserve this scope; do not interpret the first commit as an approved reduced release.

## Design
Fictional medieval living town and countryside; first/third-person; cohesive stylized realism; projectile-exclusive combat mastery, rudimentary melee only. Character origin/background customization affects equipment (including none), finances, prior experience and ties, not ultimate limits. Hunters, recruits, shepherds, apprentices, wanderers, nobles, commoners and fisher starts; acquire equipment through trade, work, factions, theft, discovery or craft.

## Disciplines
Bows (short, long, recurve, composite), crossbows (light, heavy, repeating), javelin/throwing spear, knives, axes, chakram, darts, slings/staff slings, bolas, boomerangs/throwing sticks, slingshots, blowguns, experimental launchers, alchemical containers, stones, fruit, sticks and pottery. Data-driven expansion. Each family and specialization has distinctive handling, flight, XP, levels, skill trees, knowledge and crafting; undiscovered trees revealed by acquisition or experimentation. General/family/specialization XP; skillful real outcomes, distance, precision, moving targets, trick shots, elevation, obstacles, novelty and consecutive performance; diminishing returns; no auto hit or arbitrary crit. Organic proficiency plus spendable points, knowledge/instruction gates and recipe unlocks. Standard tree through proposed level 100, uncapped mastery thereafter; no permanent mutually exclusive cap. Power draw, zoom, quick draw, slow motion and triple shot are contextual abilities limited by stamina, focus, equipment or ammunition; explicit gameplay abstractions, no hidden steering. Individual equipment quality, components, condition, ownership history, familiarity.

## Physics and world
Fixed simulation time, collision sweeps and continuous rigid-body detection; drag, gravity, wind, rotation, lift, elasticity, penetration, rebound, constraints, flexible cords, breakage, fictional materials/reactions. Different mechanics for draw, loading, throwing, rotation, rhythm, breathing and elastic tension. Actual physical interaction events determine results. Alchemical experiments with fictional ingredients, thermal/vapor/adhesive/energetic effects and persistent journal. Invention, discovery, crafting, experiments and physical chain reactions. Replay free/follow cameras, timeline, slow motion, trajectory and impact diagnostics; isolated no-XP unrestricted testing. Public ranges, military training, private yards/workshops, experiments, competitions, records.

## Life and movement
Market, residential, military, artisan and poor districts, walls, forest, farm, outlying settlement. Accessible selected interiors, roofs, doors, storage, sitting, handling, weighted walk/sprint/crouch/jump, climbing, vaulting, balancing, inspections, recovery. NPCs with possessions, occupations, routines, relationships, witnesses, rumors, faction reputation, reactive behavior and proximity LOD. Light hunger/sleep, accommodation/rent, money, upkeep and weather. Hunting, military work, artisans, merchants, criminal networks, independent employment. Armor, injury, recoverable projectiles, theft, fines, prison, customizable casual/standard/hardcore/ironman consequences. Persistent state, inventory, recipes, skills, relationships, property, records.

## Approved stack
Babylon.js WebGL2 (optional WebGPU), Havok WASM rigid-body physics, Recast navigation; specialized projectile solvers, fixed timestep, data registries, entity-component architecture, hierarchical NPC states, skeletal animation/blending/IK, glTF/GLB, Web Audio, IndexedDB, desktop/controller/touch, Playwright and simulation tests. Prioritize existing repo assets; inspect compatibility and approve newly generated asset benchmark before expansion. Initial targets 60 FPS desktop/30 mobile, 750k/250k rendered triangles, 250/120 draws, 24/12 animated nearby NPCs, 25/15 MB initial compressed transfer (targets not measurements). Release only after gameplay/visual/performance tests, catalog checks and actual GitHub Pages route verification.
