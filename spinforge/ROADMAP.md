# Roadmap
Complete-first workflow; priorities do not impose intermediate approval gates.
1. Verify actual WebGL, models and stadium contacts, mobile layout, inputs, persistence, replay and finish flow; capture genuine primary gameplay cover.
2. Physical fidelity: compare Rapier/Jolt against numerical reference, contact manifolds, mass/COM/inertia, precession/nutation, passive jumping, detachment and simultaneous finishes. Current 2.5D solver is not full 3D.
3. Asset-first: authentic reference geometry and licensed reusable assets; representative benchmark before expanding, material distinctions, contact wear and sound. No unapproved box art.
4. Complete workshop: component inventory, compatibility, physical modifications, exploded views, paid repairs by component, selectable opponent, scoring and launch-only mode improvements. Preserve baseline.
5. Replay: scrubbing, interpolation, event/effect reproduction, free camera, 0.05x–1x, saved/exportable recordings and deeper diagnostics.
6. Add catalog card only after verified gameplay cover and manifest; run project and catalog audits; merge and verify exact live version.
