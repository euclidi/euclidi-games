# Specification

- Seven-row, 28-cube triangular grid; four diagonal moves; hop arc and discrete compression frame.
- Three lives, falls and enemy contact, rescue discs, score and local best.
- Rollers, chasers and color erasers; one-touch, two-touch and cyclic tiles at rounds 1, 4 and 7.
- Responsive portrait/landscape, one directional pad, keyboard and standard controller mapping, pause and mute.
- Native 240 × 256 portrait raster, nearest-neighbor scaling, flat palette and original 24 × 24 mascot frames. Reference: original Gottlieb 1982 arcade screenshots; no original game sprites reused.
