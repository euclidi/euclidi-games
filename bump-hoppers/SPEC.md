# Specification

- Discrete triangular grid, four diagonal moves; hop arc, landing squash and recovery.
- Three lives, falls and enemy contact, rescue discs, score and local best.
- Increasing board size, rollers, chasers and color erasers; one-touch, two-touch and cyclic tiles at rounds 1, 4 and 7.
- Responsive portrait/landscape, one directional pad, keyboard and standard controller mapping, pause and mute.
- Clay-style raster textures generated at startup; not photographic stop-motion.
