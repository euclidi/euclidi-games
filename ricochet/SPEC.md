# RICOCHET — approved specification 1.0
Retro pixel art; classic arcade physics; efficiency and scoring objectives; traditional power-ups. Eight modes: campaign, endless, efficiency, high score, incremental, daily challenge, sandbox, editor. Five worlds with 10 handcrafted levels each. Animated bitmap art, traditional pegs and moving bucket, eight powers (guide, multiball, fireball, flippers, wide bucket, precision, extra ball, score rush), trick shots and scoring. 120 Hz continuous collision detection, consistent aim prediction, stuck recovery and slow motion. Original music/ambient/SFX with separate controls. Keyboard, pointer, touch, gamepad, remapping, accessibility palettes and scaling. Local persistence and versioned level import/export. Approved PixiJS 8, Rapier 2D WASM, TypeScript, Vite, IndexedDB, Web Audio, Vitest/Playwright and GitHub Pages. Complete-first implementation, playtest and disclose gaps. Current implementation uses browser ES modules rather than TS/Vite; see STATUS.

## Revision 1.1 — user-requested finale and presentation extension
Professional arcade-console GUI; each campaign board gets a distinct original synthesized arrangement in its world's instrumental style; every tenth campaign board becomes a named multi-hit boss encounter. Last-target camera detects approaching balls including misses, interpolates a bounded zoom and time rate, restores normal view/time after a miss, then on a real final hit reveals five physically divided bonus scoring channels (10k/25k/100k/25k/10k) and awards based on ball landing. Reduced-motion mode bypasses zoom/slowdown. Original music, not copied Peggle music.

## Art Direction Revision 2.0 — approved
Miniature Worlds with Retro Pre-rendered CGI influence; pre-rendered 3D plus dynamic lighting; mixed roster, two starting companions and five earned by defeating campaign bosses. Remove every manual slow-motion input. Simplify menu flow, rebuild assets, reflective materials and environmental animation. Preserve modes, level soundtrack, bosses, physics and Fever. Blender-produced assets and full lighting fidelity remain required production goals; V0.4 uses an interim analytic precomputation renderer because Blender is unavailable.

## Responsive presentation amendment
Automatically select desktop, tablet, mobile portrait or mobile landscape presentation based on usable viewport, orientation and input capabilities; no user-agent assumptions or manual mode selection required. Maintain same gameplay world and input mapping when layout changes. Touch-first controls, safe-area padding, compact companion and HUD, preserve logical collision coordinates.
