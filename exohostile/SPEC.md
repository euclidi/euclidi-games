# Revision 1.0 — approved scope
Grounded science fiction; native device pixel resolution; first-person alien energy-testing facility with fictional Earth-themed exhibits, printed human-shaped inert boards and synthetic mannequins.

## Facility
One connected environment: armory, proving range, material laboratory, gravity chamber, workshop, sandbox chamber and Earth archive. Consistent scale and navigable transitions; machinery, signage and material detail.

## Weapons and targets
Separate plasma, arc and gravity devices, distinct models, alien ergonomics, animated cells, thermal behavior, adjustable output, inertia, inspection and optional assisted handling. Plasma projectiles and thermal residue; arc charge and conduction; attraction, repulsion and field manipulation. Interchangeable board, reactive mechanism, articulated mannequin, material and mixed configurations. Persistent impact marks, mechanical motion, replaceable components and material-specific reactions.

## Modes
Unrestricted sandbox, technician contracts and equipment progression, persistent supplies, wear, maintenance and damage. Shared simulation. Object spawn, reposition, duplication and experiment presets. Physics/material/energy inspection.

## Technology
Three.js/WebGL 2, Rapier WASM, TypeScript, Blender/glTF, PBR shaders, KTX2/Basis, skeletal animation/IK, Web Audio spatial sound, IndexedDB, Vite and GitHub Pages. Asset-first selection and compatibility checks; keyboard/mouse, gamepad and touch. Native rendering with settings. All implementation and verification deviations must be recorded.
