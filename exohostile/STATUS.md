## Approximated / incomplete
- **Art:** custom beveled/primitive meshes, simple anatomy and grip, generated signage and synthetic environment reflections. No authored Blender/glTF weapon or alien rig, detailed texture atlases, KTX2 pipeline, IK, production animation or curated high-detail asset pack. Optional remote CC0 floor texture only; license reviewed but actual download not tested.
- **Physics:** plasma uses an initial ray hit and visual flight, not continuous projectile collision. Arc propagation is proximity-based; gravity uses impulses rather than sustained fields/suspension. Thermal, electrical and structural effects use scalar approximations; no liquid simulation, realistic perforation, conductive topology or full fracture model. Jointed mannequin parts are not detachable through damage. Mechanical targets have hinges but no full motor-driven carriers or machinery maintenance.
- **Gameplay:** generic repeatable specimen-test assignments; currency/unlock/service loop is minimal; persistent mode saves some transforms and board marks but cannot fully restore broken specimens, fragments, joints, or facility damage. Facility damage, comprehensive repair economics, component upgrades, per-weapon inventory, malfunctions, and persistent environmental destruction remain missing. Mode-specific independent save slots are written but only latest save is exposed in UI. No full experiment preset editor.
- **Interface/audio:** terminal is an overlay rather than full diegetic interaction; simple synthesized non-spatial audio, no authored sound bank; no fully animated weapon displays or detailed inspection tools; no configurable graphics-quality settings; controller/mobile ergonomics untested.
- **Architecture:** plain ES module JavaScript rather than TypeScript/Vite source pipeline; dependencies remote rather than vendored. No tests beyond syntax checks. Production deployment is contingent on existing Pages configuration and publication.
