# NEREID K-07 — consolidated scope
Preserve all original requirements: first-person walkable vessel, man/unman helm, propulsion, ballast, heading, depth, optical mast, bridge, sonar, observation, galley, crew quarters, washroom and engine compartment. Live sonar contacts, diverse animated marine animals, deep-water visibility and adjustable lights, deployable manipulators for samples and lighting, machinery/water/radio/footstep sound, keyboard, touch and browser Gamepad API with configurable generic axes/buttons. Native DirectInput access cannot be promised in browsers.

Target: a unified navigable ocean and ship, with stable world-space geography, realistic relative motion, bounded hydrodynamics/ballast, coherent collision, articulated arms, depth-dependent ecology, sound, high-quality assets and materials. Real exterior diving is an outstanding requirement. Gameplay should arise from exploration, operating stations, observation and recovering samples, not unrelated minigames. Persistence and voyage logging are supporting additions, not replacements for original scope.

Architecture: preserve existing Three.js WebGL source; use fixed-step simulation and explicit ship/world coordinate transforms; investigate Rapier for collision/rigid bodies after compatibility and performance validation, not a wholesale engine migration. Web Audio and Gamepad APIs. Prefer licensed reusable glTF and PBR assets, with per-asset license/scale/rig/LOD checks. Candidate CC0 material source: https://polyhaven.com/ (not yet integrated).
