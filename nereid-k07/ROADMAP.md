# Continuous development priorities
These are internal workstreams, not approval gates. Implement and test cumulatively.
1. Startup and browser smoke tests, mobile fit, all input modes, collision and performance.
2. Replace repeated terrain with deterministic geographic chunks, depth/bathymetry and seabed clearance; model buoyancy, drag, steering, ballast and depth hold; add persistent navigation and sample fields.
3. Asset-first overhaul: licensed submarine equipment, materials and marine rigs. Audit alignment, scale, animation, LOD and lighting; retain existing rooms and stations.
4. Live contact acquisition, range, bearing and tracking; continuous animal movement, habitat and avoidance; IK manipulators, sample mass, collisions and gripper ownership; exterior diving and return.
5. Spatial audio, functional interior interactions, voyage save/load and recovery, exploration objectives and final performance/accessibility review.
Release gate: actual browser playtest of all stations, collision/controls, sample capture and release, voyage navigation and depth transitions; no startup errors; catalog screenshot from real gameplay; direct play URL checked.
