# Night Watch — Roadmap

1. Verify standalone browser deployment, mobile layout and physical controllers.
2. Playtest enemy pacing, encounter design, weapons and difficulty; refine pixel art and sound.
3. Expand levels, environmental storytelling and combat variety only after establishing a polished baseline. No later features are represented as implemented.

Graphics follow-up: visually validate the 0.2.0 material/lighting revision on desktop and mobile; replace remaining low-resolution actor and pickup artwork with higher-detail directional sprite sets, and tune frame budgets against real devices.
