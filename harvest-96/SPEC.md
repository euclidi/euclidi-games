# Approved scope

Hybrid farm-restoration campaign and endless occupational sandbox; faithful but refined 1996 visual aesthetic; progressively unlocked world; agriculture with soil moisture, fertility, crop rotation, pests, disease and quality; genetics, breeding, aging and animal care; climate and seasons; autonomous community with households, schedules, finances, aging and population; dynamic supply/demand, businesses and production chains; balanced character needs and skills; property and building customization; living ecosystem, wildlife and fishing; rural occupations; immersive pixel-art controls with optional analytical tools. Keyboard, controller and mobile. Static deployment via Euclidi Games, only after browser QA and catalog verification. No proprietary game graphics.
