# Third-party assets

- Three.js r150 (MIT), https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r150 ; vendor/LICENSE. Official GLTFLoader and BufferGeometryUtils from same tag, with local bridge for UMD build.
- Kenney Graveyard Kit (CC0 1.0), https://kenney.nl/assets/graveyard-kit ; distribution mirror https://github.com/Hidencod/tge-assets/tree/main/packs/graveyard-kit . gravestone-bevel.glb, gravestone-round.glb, gravestone-cross.glb. Bounds normalization and dimmed material colors at runtime. In-browser decode and appearance pending verification.
- Other meshes and audio retain existing procedural content; not third-party replacement.
