# Asset provenance

All artwork in art.mjs is original pixel-cluster data authored for The Meeting Seam. No third-party asset files are included. The atlas is rendered into transparent RGBA bitmaps at load time; poses are composed with bitmap layers. These are interim sprites and not a claim of meeting the approved visual quality. The OpenGameArt mining packs were researched but not incorporated. Future art must be visually inspected and licensed before inclusion.
