# Scope and specification

The target is a charming, dense, high-resolution bitmap mining sandbox with physically credible mining, independently motivated autonomous workers, varied geology, irregular excavation, material transport, structural support and real directional headlamps. The player may aid or disrupt either worker without directly controlling them. Incremental progression must arise from extracted material and equipment improvements. Feet must plant, strikes must contact rock, and carts must obey transport constraints. No visible block terrain, SVG-like character silhouettes or primitive art substitutes. The current browser port uses a fine density field, a fixed-step agent model, Canvas bitmap compositing, approximate span failure, and interim authored text-mask sprites. A higher fidelity renderer, authored animation atlas and validated physics are still required. No final stack approval implied by this port.
