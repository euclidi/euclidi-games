# Lunar Angler — approved specification, Revision 3.0
Approved by owner on 2026-11-27. This is the target scope, not a claim of implementation. Supersedes revisions 1.0 and 2.0 only where changed.

## Experience
Sit on a moon and cast a hook through a miniature continuously simulated universe. Gravity assists emerge from moving masses. Fishing line connects the same equipment, hook and creatures in all views. Both traditional elastic rod and slingshot mechanisms; both orbital atlas and navigable lunar/3D views plus hook-following view. Handcrafted and seeded procedural systems/galaxies; familiar and extraterrestrial species. Expedition progression and unrestricted sandbox. No artificial daily rewards or energy gates.
Full loop: observe, prepare, cast, navigate, submerge, attract fish, manage tension and reel, collect, discover and improve. Observatory, atlas, workshop, aquarium, bestiary, campsite customization, optional contracts and achievements. Equipment includes rods, reels, hooks, lines, weights, bobbers and bait with distinct physical properties. Autonomous schooling, feeding, predation, migration, territories, curiosity and avoidance.

## Approved simulation
C++/WASM authoritative double precision core in a worker; optional GPU compute, never mandatory. Custom adaptive hybrid orbital integrator benchmarked against REBOUND/IAS15; symplectic stable-orbit path, adaptive high-order encounter path, synchronized tethered integration; normalized compressed distances. Moving masses, rotation, binary systems, atmospheric entry, gravity assists, collision, capture and escape; independent predictor and safe time acceleration.
Direct Cosserat rod plus variable-length XPBD tether; adaptive discretization, conservation-aware remeshing, finite propagation, friction, snag/wrap, abrasion, self-contact and breakage. Elastic launch energy, mechanical torque, spool inertia, drag and physical payout. Hybrid adaptive aquatic solvers: global tides/currents/waves/weather, regional pressure/salinity/temperature and local SPH/PBF/MLS-MPM as appropriate, with CPU authoritative gameplay forces; buoyancy, drag, lift, added mass. Hybrid articulated and soft-body biomechanics; actual creature propulsion and resistance. Continuous collision and stable coupled substeps. 120 Hz nominal.

## Approved visuals
Celestial Clockwork primary, bitmap science fiction, physical miniature universe and astronomical naturalism secondary. Engraved brass, worn wood, optical glass, exposed mechanical parts, naturalistic terrain and aquatic anatomy. Mixed specialized rendering: Three.js WebGL2, optional WebGPU, optimized glTF, PBR/baked maps, authored bitmaps, spherical LOD, atmospheric scattering, reflective/refractive oceans and underwater absorption/caustics, animated creatures. Astronomical lighting with inverse-square falloff, phases, eclipse shadows, reflection and multi-star lighting. 1280-pixel internal reference width, viewport-dependent height, optional quality scaling without changing gameplay. Hybrid instrument UI with readable conventional controls. Spatial audio, mechanical cues, atmospheric score. Mouse/keyboard, touch, gamepad, remapping and optional haptics. Desktop and mobile no scroll/stretch.

## Acceptance
REBOUND energy drift <0.01% over 100 defined test orbits, encounter error <0.1% against IAS15. Unloaded cable error <0.1%; catenary/rod equilibrium within 2%; buoyancy within 1%, terminal velocity within 2% of analytical results. No tunneling in defined tests. Account for line length, mass, energy and momentum; state unchanged by camera switching. Stable saves. 60 fps desktop, 30–60 fps mobile targets. Real gameplay, complete scope, original or licensed assets and visual inspection. Complete-first implementation, then iterative improvement. Publish in existing Euclidi Games catalog after playtesting; disclose every outstanding requirement.
