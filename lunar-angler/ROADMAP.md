# Roadmap
All approved features are in scope; these are internal work packages, not user approval gates.
1. Numerical fidelity: REBOUND reference suite, adaptive high-order encounter integration, true 3D authoritative state, direct Cosserat + XPBD, variable resolution, conservation and continuous collision. Add analytical catenary, bending, drag and buoyancy benchmarks.
2. Coupled mechanics: actual rod swing and flex, spool inertia and material tension, equipment properties, friction, wrap and self-contact, robust retrieval, atmosphere and prediction.
3. Aquatic simulation: CPU authoritative buoyancy/drag/currents, local adaptive fluid solver and optional GPU refinements, articulated/soft-body swimmers, behavior and bait.
4. Full progression and universe: authored starter, procedural galaxies/binaries, observatory, workshop, aquarium, campsite, bestiary, contracts, achievements, reliable full save/load and sandbox.
5. Asset and UX production: textured bespoke clockwork and character models, bitmap journals, detailed planets/sea life, physically based lighting, specialized atmosphere/water passes, spatial audio, responsive layouts and accessible controls; inspect all camera views and obtain approval before cover publication.
6. Verification: test every public launch path, desktop/mobile input, gameplay loop, performance and numerical acceptance targets in SPEC.md. Document benchmark results, licenses, known failures and deployment version.
