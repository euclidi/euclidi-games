# Status — v0.1 experimental (2026-11-27)
**Revision 3.0 is NOT complete.** This is a partial playable core/build, not a certified release.

## Implemented
Freestanding C++ compiled to WASM, double precision, 120 Hz requested step in worker; mutual softened gravitational attraction and kick-drift-kick integration with extra near-body substeps; 48-node unilateral iterative XPBD cable, line payout/retrieval, point contact and simplified damage; two elastic launch-energy settings; water-region drag/gravity reduction, 12 moving fish with simple radial/tangential behavior, proximity attachment and recovery. Seed variation in five-body systems. Three.js shared-state atlas/surface/hook views, procedural raster surface textures, simple modeled clockwork props/fish, star point illumination, 1280-wide internal frame, layout CSS, sliders and keyboard/touch/gamepad bindings, simple synthesized effects, specimen journal and localStorage progression record; expedition gate and unrestricted sandbox access to new seeds.

## Missing / partial / unverified
- Orbital: no REBOUND comparison, no adaptive high-order encounter integrator or orbital error benchmarks, no binary stars/true galaxies, body rotation not authoritative, no atmosphere entry model, independent prediction or time acceleration; aim line is direction only. No full 3D physical simulation (planar core).
- Structural: standard iterative XPBD only, not direct Cosserat; no dynamic remeshing, actual traditional swing/rod flex, spool inertia/gear torque, true drag/friction, continuous collision, self-contact, robust wrapping, conservation or material benchmarks. Cable and fish contacts are approximations. No validated tension units; scale is normalized.
- Aquatic: simplified drag and gravity reduction rather than calibrated buoyancy, no hybrid SPH/PBF/MLS-MPM or GPU compute, tides, regional currents, waves, weather, pressure/salinity/temperature, lift, added mass or fluid coupling. Fish use simple steering, not articulated/soft-body force-generating biomechanics; no complete ecosystem, bait or species-specific anatomy/behavior.
- Gameplay: basic cast/fish/retrieve and seed progression only; full equipment selection/upgrades, aquarium, workshop, campsite customization, contracts, achievements, observatory discovery, complete procedural galaxies and state save/load not implemented. LocalStorage only saves journal/credits/mode; no IndexedDB or complete state restoration. No remapping/haptics.
- Art: original procedural textures and assembled geometry, not approved production-quality custom models or authored pixel atlases; no sophisticated water/atmosphere/caustics, real eclipses/reflection, binary illumination, dynamic shadow system, proper retro asset pipeline, high-detail seated character or advanced instrument animation. No visual inspection or screenshot approval. No approved cover art added.
- Performance: no adaptive rendering profiles or GPU acceleration yet, no real device FPS data, no scientific accuracy certification; numerical acceptance targets not verified. Browser smoke, mobile, gamepad, audio and deployed-version checks outstanding.
