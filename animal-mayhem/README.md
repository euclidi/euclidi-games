# Animal Mayhem
Development build 0.1.0 (unpublished). Mobile landscape indoor cat sandbox. Move with left analog; tap A to jump, hold A to pounce; tap B to interact or swat, hold B to carry. Keyboard: WASD/arrows, Space, E. Journal revisits unlocked homes. No aging past adulthood or mandatory survival chores.

Three.js 0.170.0, Rapier 0.17.3, glTF, Web Audio, IndexedDB. Source assets are remotely loaded from a pinned revision of [find-the-cat](https://github.com/druboni/find-the-cat/tree/0ee6eb153807ebff3c50cdff5778830b74e36b5a).
