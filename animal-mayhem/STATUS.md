# Status — 0.1.0 development branch
Implemented in source: five configurations with age and size, real external GLB loading, Rapier floor and furniture colliders and dynamic props, kinematic movement, assisted leaps, carry and swat, discovery progression and replay, touch and keyboard input, IndexedDB campaign records, basic synthesized feedback, tracking camera, credits.
Asset handoff: copies of the existing source collection, Three.js 0.170.0 and Rapier 0.17.3 are in `assets/` and `vendor/` for the next developer. The existing game still uses its original remote URLs; these copies are not wired in. Candidate animal models and their provenance are documented in `assets/README.md`; these are not integrated gameplay. The original cat GLB has no skin or animation clips. The upstream furniture source is an informed identification, not a confirmed model-by-model provenance audit.

Not complete: five homes are single-room configurations, not full households; cat movement uses a procedural bob; no true fracturing, cloth, liquids, ambient animal encounters, distinct post-campaign pet gameplay, fully realized everyday needs benefits, persistent object transforms or fully adaptive camera transitions. Candidate models require visual-harmony, animation, scale and contact checks; the sugar glider still has no selected redistributable model. Mobile input needs full playtesting. No catalog entry or deployment, and no cover until a real screenshot is captured and reviewed. Do not describe this as the completed scope.
