# Approved specification
Mobile landscape analog + A/B; stylized harmonious 3D interiors; adaptive automatic camera; five households, kitten to adult; rare incidental people/animals; no outside focus. Assisted movement, physical mayhem (swat, jump, pounce, grab, scratch, climb), optional eating/sleep/grooming/play benefits, natural growth plus signature challenges and discovery; no punishing needs, old age or mortality. Complete campaign by experiencing all five homes, growing into adulthood and completing selected challenges. Unlock other indoor pets after completion; cat remains primary lifetime campaign. Replay previously unlocked homes with distinct species abilities and exclusive interactions. Eventual roster includes dog, ferret, parrot, rabbit, rat, sugar glider, tarantula, gecko, fish, snake, hamster and other indoor pets. Use real licensed assets, Three.js/WebGL, Rapier, skeletal animation, procedural adjustment, Web Audio, IndexedDB, touch controls and Euclidi Games deployment. Do not silently simplify or mark incomplete features complete.
