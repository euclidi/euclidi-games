# Scope and acceptance criteria

Preserve seven original specimens: can, incandescent bulb, watermelon, toaster, toy robot, CRT television and rubber duck. Preserve probe placement, power control, moisture, ground return, capacitor, inspection, slow motion, persistent experiment damage, debris, sound, score and touchscreen/keyboard controls.

Target: component-level connected circuits, local electrical/thermal/material failures, persistent collidable fragments, spatial moisture, multiple specimen workbench, event-linked audio, detailed authored assets, discovery-based records and responsive controls. Fictional electrical parameters; do not present as engineering simulation.

V3 is a transitional enhancement of the existing Canvas 2D build, not a finished physics engine. Future architecture: independent circuit/thermal solver; evaluate mature 2D rigid-body engine (Rapier2D, Matter.js or Planck.js) against browser compatibility and collision requirements; evaluate sprite renderer (PixiJS) with licensed authored raster assets. Verify dependencies and licenses before adoption. Avoid replacing baseline with reduced demo.
