Controls and development notes

Open and remove pits by clicking the avocados twice. Knife: draw a stroke completely through a piece. Spoon: start on food, drag, release over the bowl or plate. Masher: hold and move over avocado. Fold: sweep the spatula through the mixture. Lime and salt: hold over the bowl. Dip: lift a smaller bite with a chip. Arrow keys or left gamepad stick move a virtual pointer; space or gamepad south button uses the tool. Number keys 1–8 select tools. Camera rotation and zoom are independent of food state.

R3 development preview: actual CC0 source models; reduced-order food parcels, not a continuous fluid solver. Recorded audio, exact ingredient models and final retro-CGI art pass remain unfinished. See project status for verification.
