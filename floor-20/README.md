# Floor 20 — Reclaimed

An atmospheric first-person environmental sandbox in Westbridge Plaza's abandoned east tower. Explore floors 19–21, inspect furnishings and equipment, manipulate portable objects, and investigate the office's remaining machinery. The Scene Viewer remains the default; use Walk or Enter Room to explore.

## Play
[Development preview](https://euclidi.github.io/euclidi-games/floor-20/?v=15)

## Controls
- Viewer: drag to orbit, right drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
- Walk: WASD, drag to look, Shift to sprint, E to use, F to inspect, G to collect, I for field kit, R to rotate, T to throw. Settings include remapping and controller profiles.
- Mobile: on-screen controls and drag look. Browser Gamepad API support; native DirectInput is not exposed.

This is the cumulative full WebGL project, not a recreated miniature. See [SPEC](SPEC.md), [STATUS](STATUS.md), and [ROADMAP](ROADMAP.md). No external assets or libraries were added in v15. The release is a development preview, not a completed simulator.
