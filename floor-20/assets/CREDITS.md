Fabric036 is a woven procedural textile asset, used as an approximate carpet/upholstery substitute, not a scanned carpet. Diffuse maps only; not full PBR. Runtime image resampling is 512px on coarse-pointer devices, 1024px otherwise. Audio decodes after a user gesture, with surface-dependent footsteps, distance attenuation and stereo positioning. No ambient loops yet.
