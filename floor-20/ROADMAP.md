# Development workflow

The complete-first workflow applies to the entire accepted scope. Internal dependencies organize development, not recurring approval gates. Preserve the cumulative project; make incremental changes, test, document, and commit each substantial pass. No substitute miniature is permitted.

1. Resolve real-browser startup and verify current preview on desktop and mobile, including shader compile, settings, room entry, door traversal, stairs, placement, specimen, POST and rendering performance.
2. Asset audit and integration: replace weak models with licensed compatible assets or carefully crafted geometry where needed. Improve material and lighting fidelity while retaining layout. Select validated asset pipeline before changing renderer.
3. Complete robust contacts and physics; evaluate mature solver integration and regression-test all current interactions. Add credible tool and computer component mechanics, destruction, sound and world persistence.
4. Improve tactile locomotion, seated interactions, reflection, inspection and UI; verify full scope, measure performance, playtest with actual devices, and only then remove the unverified preview label.

The published preview is the current development checkpoint, not a claim that these acceptance requirements are satisfied.
