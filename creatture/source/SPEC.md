# Creatture — approved Revision 3.0
Approved 2026-11-27, consolidating R1, R2 and R3. No scope reduction authorized.

## Experience
GROW-inspired order-dependent evolution puzzle plus autonomous artificial life. Twelve foundational elements (membrane, nutrients, water, light, minerals, muscle, nerves, microbes, spores, heat, catalyst, resonance); world-specific additions; authored climactic sequences, alternatives, hidden outcomes, no universal superiority score. Three modes: finite deterministic puzzle, free cultivation with dose/placement/exposure/time controls, spoiler-free living archive with histories, lineage, breeding and cross-world transfer. Continuous idle activity independent of puzzle time. Six complete worlds: Primordium (functional anatomy), Abyss (pressure, currents, buoyancy, swimming), Mycelium (distributed nutrient networks, spores/decomposition), Silica (mineral metabolism/crystalline growth), Prosthesis (electrical organs, actuators, neural interfaces/rejection), Communion (multi-species ecology, competition/cooperation/predation/parasitism/mutualism). Full environments, specialized mechanics and distinct soundscapes.
