# Pull It Out — development 0.1

A toy-box physics sandbox for extracting flexible novelty shapes from mechanical sockets. No characters or sexual acts. The mechanical claw aims directly, lowers and raises through motor-like smoothing, twists, grips and drops objects into a tray.

## Controls

Pointer/touch aim; WASD move; Q/E lower/lift; Z/X twist; Space or grip button toggle; wheel changes height. Gamepad left stick, triggers, shoulder buttons and south face button correspond to those operations. Sandbox settings and optional 80-second challenge are in the workbench.

## Dependencies and provenance

Reuses the repository's `guacamole/vendor/three.module.min.js` and `rapier.es.js` with their adjacent MIT and Apache/MIT license notices. Custom geometry for socket board, claw and stylized objects, original canvas labels and synthesized sounds. No external model pack acquired or claimed.
