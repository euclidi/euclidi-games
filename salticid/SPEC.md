# Scope / revision 1
Preserve the entire established scope: a detailed jumping spider, eight anatomically articulated legs, weight and inertia, continuous ballistic jumps, landing suspension, safety silk, swinging and reeling, reactive flies, stalking, pouncing, prey grasping and spinning, walkable silk bridges and protective retreats. Connected miniature natural environment, resource meters, exploration, keyboard/touch/gamepad controls, camera orbit, audio, direct browser play in Euclidi Games. Jumping spider silk should model draglines and retreats rather than an orb-web species.
Architecture: Three.js rendering; custom fixed-step contact/ballistic/rope and procedural IK model retained from original build; evaluate Rapier for three-dimensional collision, XPBD for compliant silk and contact constraints. Do not substitute a simpler 2D game. Asset-first policy: inspect license, scale, rig, geometry and performance before adopting; target real anatomy and textured natural surfaces. Full fidelity is not claimed by the current development revision.
Acceptance: no airborne pause when holding leap, fixed-length leg chains within tolerance, verified supported contacts, damping and traction, no clipping/tether anchor misalignment, natural prey steering and grappling, persistent anchored silk, stable responsive camera and controls, complete play loop, desktop/mobile/browser/gamepad tests and actual deployed URL verification.
