JUMPING SPIDER · FIELD NOTES

Walk: Hold ◀ / ▶ (or A / D) to walk, each of eight legs plants and pushes in alternating groups. The spider has momentum, drag, torso suspension and uneven terrain response.

Jump: While perched, hold LEAP (Space) to crouch and charge, release to launch. Once airborne, holding it cannot pause or freeze gravity. Aim at a fly by tapping it first. Slight directional correction is possible in air.

Silk: Every jump spins a safety dragline. Tap SILK (E) to secure to the nearest anchor. Swing by moving left/right. Hold SILK to reel upward; tap SILK again briefly to release.

Hunt: STALK (F) reduces speed and noise. Flies have velocity, steering limits, wingbeats, and flee from rapid approaches. Collide mid-pounce to seize and wrap prey.

Weave: Tap WEAVE (Q) while perched. Hold still to build a silk retreat; walk to a different platform and toggle off to place a draped bridge. Silk is consumed and slowly recovered.

Camera: Drag the scenery to orbit slightly; tap a fly to select it. Sound can be enabled after a gesture.
