# Scope — revision 1.0

Recovered from the established conversation and v5 baseline; not an expansion into a new game genre.

- First-person exploration of a coherent candy sewer, with continuous vault, caramel canal, three accessible bridges, Strawberry Trunk, Jelly Pump Gallery, Fondant Reservoir, Licorice Engine House, Sugar Crystal Filters and seven viewpoints.
- Cel-shaded lighting throughout; deliberate model silhouettes, accurate scale, supported pipes, mounted hardware, attached mechanical parts, no visible crude primitive placeholders or misalignments. Primitive construction geometry may be hidden or naturally appropriate. Artistic acceptance requires rendered inspection.
- Working waffle sluice, valve, animated lift cables, pumps, conveyor, gears, four collectible samples; optional ambient audio and distance-driven footsteps.
- Camera-correct movement, acceleration/deceleration, body footprint, sliding along boundaries, consistent gait and adjustable/reduced bob; keyboard, mouse/touch and browser gamepad support, including raw/DirectInput-style devices with configurable axes.
- Preserve existing scope. Use asset-first acquisition/reuse with license and compatibility audit, custom modeling only where necessary, complete-first implementation and iterative correction.
- Browser destination: Euclidi Games GitHub Pages. Maintain a playable full-scene build and a clear status record. No unrelated content, NPCs, combat or management mechanics were assumed.

Acceptance: verify every route and interaction in a rendered browser, inspect every room and attachment, check controller on physical hardware and mobile layout, profile rendering, verify persistence and reset, then promote preview to validated release.
